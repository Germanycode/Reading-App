# Lumina Reader Design System

## Visual Identity
A distraction-free reading experience that bridges the gap between digital precision and the warmth of physical books. The system prioritizes long-form legibility through intentional whitespace and high-fidelity typography.

## Typography
- **Primary Serif (Reading Content)**: EB Garamond / Playfair Display (depending on mode)
  - *Headline Large*: 48px, Bold, tracking-tight
  - *Body Reading*: 20px, Regular, leading-relaxed
- **UI Sans-Serif (Navigation/Meta)**: Inter / Helvetica
  - *Label Small*: 12px, Medium, uppercase, tracking-widest

## Color Palette (Sepia Theme)
- **Surface**: `#f4ecd8` (Warm aged paper)
- **On-Surface (Primary Text)**: `#433422` (Deep charcoal brown)
- **On-Surface Variant (Secondary)**: `#6d5d4b` (Muted earth tone)
- **Accent**: `#705d49` (Subtle leather-like brown for interactions)
- **Surface Container**: `#ebe2c9` (Defined structural areas)

## Component Principles
- **Side Navigation**: Fixed width (240px-280px), minimal icons, focus on section hierarchy.
- **Top App Bar**: Transparent or matching surface background, floating utility icons.
- **Content Container**: Maximum width (800px) centered for optimal line length.
- **Interactive States**: Subtle opacity shifts (80% to 100%) rather than high-contrast color changes.

## Spacing & Layout
- **Gutter**: 32px (Desktop)
- **Stack Spacing**: 24px (Large), 12px (Medium), 8px (Small)
- **Corner Radius**: 8px (Soft, organic feel)
